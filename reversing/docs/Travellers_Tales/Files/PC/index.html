PC Windows Filesystem & Binary Notes

Inspected Directly + Reference: LSW2 PC, TCS Steam, BM2 Steam, and 2024 HP Collection x64 (.datwin); LEGO Worlds noted via reference parsers.

Windows PC releases use Direct3D 9 (x86) / Direct3D 11 (x64) with little-endian F32 vertex buffers inside big-endian HSEM chunks and replace console audio (.msf/.xma) with .ogg/.wav.

PC Specific Anomalies

  • TCS Steam GAME.DAT (v-3) stores ascending integer IDs (0x3c27d...) in place of FNV1a-32 hashes.
  • 2024 Double Eleven x64 remasters replace .DAT with .datwin (0x12345678 sentinel + LZHAM) and .pkiwin/.pkdwin (zlib).
