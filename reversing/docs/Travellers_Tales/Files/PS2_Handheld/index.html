PS2, GameCube & Handhelds Filesystem & Binary Notes

Inspected Directly: LSW1 & LSW2 PS2 ISOs (TOC v-1), LSW1 GC RVZ\x01, LSW2 NDS NitroFS (AL7E), and LSW1 GBA (BLWE).

Early console and handheld releases span TT's first PS2 GAME.DAT (TOC v-1 with RNC\x02 compression in LSW1 and raw assets in LSW2), GameCube GL5X4F discs, and separate Amaze/Griptonite ARM handheld engines (NDS NitroFS + GBA).
