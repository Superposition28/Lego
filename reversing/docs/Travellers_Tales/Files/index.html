1. History of the Traveller's Tales Engine & Format Evolution (2005–2024)

Across 19 years of LEGO titles, Traveller's Tales evolved from loose uncompressed Nu2 assets on Original Xbox/PS2 into monolithic hashed .DAT containers with custom lossless codecs (LZ2K, bit-2-toggled DFLT, CMP2 LZMA, Deflate_v1.0), reflection-driven gameplay schemas (EdFile), and finally shader-and-resource-graph engines (HSEM, .CC4HSER, and .CC40TAD). The table below maps every confirmed architectural transition.

2. Master Cross-Format Relationship & Dependency Graph

No TT Games format exists in isolation: runtime loaders mount .DAT/.HDR containers, resolve virtual paths via uppercase FNV1a-32 (TtPathHash), load scene/character manifests (.res / EdFile), decode HSEM or NU20 geometry, bind material texture slots, and stream skeletal/facial/audio sub-resources.
