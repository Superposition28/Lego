Xbox / Xbox 360 Filesystem & Binary Notes

Inspected Directly: LSW1 Xbox, LSW2 Xbox, LSW2 X360, plus HP 1–4 and HP 5–7 X360 executables.

Era 1–2 Xbox and early X360 discs (LSW1, LSW2) ship loose uncompressed files directly inside XDVDFS (MICROSOFT*XBOX*MEDIA), whereas Era 3+ X360 releases mount chunked GAME*.DAT containers identically to PS3.

XDVDFS & XEX2 Notes

  • Original Xbox security partition offset typically sits at 0x18300000; X360 partition offset sits at 0x0FD90000 / 0x02080000.
  • Xbox 360 executables are big-endian PPC64 PE images wrapped in XEX2.
