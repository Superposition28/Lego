PS3 Filesystem & Binary Notes

Inspected Directly: 15 Redump/decrypted PS3 disc builds across EU/US/JP regions (multi-extent ISO-9660 + PARAM.SFO + GAME*.DAT/.HDR + CMP2) plus 72 extracted .pkg / .rap PSN, Demo, DLC & Theme packages.

All 15 PS3 titles use unencrypted ISO-9660 filesystems (with multi-extent chaining for files > 4 GiB such as PAD.DAT and Indy 1/BM1/Indy 2 single-volume GAME.DAT) paired with NPDRM-signed EBOOT.BIN SELF executables. Newly inspected JP discs (BM3, Avengers) revealed regional container splits (BM3 JP .HDR/.DAT vs EU/US embedded v-6; Avengers JP omitting PAD.DAT).

PS3 PKG / RAP Distribution Census (72 Packages Across 10 Games)

Inspecting 72 extracted .pkg and .rap packages alongside the disc releases confirmed uniform Sony PS3 NPDRM packaging (0x7F504B47, big-endian item count at +0x14, Content ID at +0x30..0x54): free Demos set drm_type=3 (mostly license-free, though several EU Demos such as IJ2, HP1–4, and PotC ship drm=2 + .rap), whereas digital PSN full games (BM2, BM3, HP5–7, PotC, LOTR, Marvel 1, Avengers, Hobbit), DLC packs (BM2, LOTR, Marvel 1, Hobbit, Dimensions), and XMB Themes (Marvel 1 content_type=9) use drm_type=2 paired with 16-byte .rap license keys.

Key PS3 Takeaways

  • Hash verification reaches 100% on 14/15 disc titles (including BM3 52,155 direct + 2 collision-table entries and Avengers 81,232/81,232) and 124,712/124,716 on Dimensions.
  • Compression progresses from LZ2K (TCS..BM2) to bit-2-toggled DFLT (LOTR..Avengers), adding per-file RC4-encrypted ZIPX (5,980 scripts/text assets) in Avengers.
