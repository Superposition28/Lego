# Game Research: LEGO Pirates of the Caribbean (TT Games)

## 1. Title Overview
*LEGO Pirates of the Caribbean: The Video Game* was released in May 2011 alongside the theatrical premiere of *On Stranger Tides*. It adapted all four feature films (*The Curse of the Black Pearl*, *Dead Man's Chest*, *At World's End*, *On Stranger Tides*) and served as a direct technological contemporary of *LEGO Harry Potter: Years 5–7*.

---

## 2. Technical Features & Milestones
- **Water & Physics Simulation:** Introduced TT Games' most advanced water rendering and buoyancy physics to date, incorporating dynamic ocean wakes, underwater traversal (undead skeleton characters walking on the seabed), and ship combat.
- **Compass Tracking Mechanic:** Introduced Jack Sparrow's compass item wheel for environmental secret discovery, an item-tracking subsystem closely related to the Spectrespecs and spell discovery mechanics in *LEGO Harry Potter*.
- **Port Royal Hub:** Seamless coastal hub connecting all four film campaigns.

---

## 3. Engine & Archive Profile
- **Platform Coverage:** PlayStation 3, Xbox 360, Wii, Microsoft Windows, Mac OS X, PSP, Nintendo 3DS.
- **Archive Format:** Generation 2 `PakDat v1.01` (`GAME.DAT` .. `GAME*.DAT`).
- **PPU Executable Identity (PS3):**
  - Uses the same Sony PS3 SDK generation (`03.60` / `03.70`) as *LEGO Harry Potter: Years 5–7*.
  - Shares audio streaming formats, animation rigs, and RSX command buffer state management.
